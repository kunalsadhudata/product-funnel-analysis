# Dataset Selection and Sampling Methodology

## 1. Dataset Selection

**Dataset:** eCommerce Behavior Data from Multi-Category Store

**Source:** Kaggle — https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/mkechinov/ecommerce-behavior-data-from-multi-category-store

**Selected file:** `2019-Oct.csv`

**Analysis period:** October 1–31, 2019 (UTC)

**Original dataset size:** 42,448,764 event records

## 2. Business Relevance

The dataset contains customer interaction events from an e-commerce platform.

The available event types include:

- `view` — Product viewing
- `cart` — Product added to cart
- `purchase` — Purchase event

These events support analysis of product engagement, cart activity, purchasing behavior, and conversion drop-offs.

The dataset also contains product identifiers, category information, brand, price, customer identifiers, and session identifiers.

## 3. Full Dataset Profiling

The complete October dataset was examined using pandas chunk processing.

The dataset contains all 31 calendar days of October 2019.

Important missing-value observations:

- `user_session`: 2 missing records
- Core fields including `event_time`, `event_type`, `product_id`, `price`, and `user_id` contain no missing values.

## 4. Sampling Strategy

A deterministic customer-level sampling approach was selected to reduce computational requirements while preserving observed customer event histories.

Customer selection was based on a reproducible SHA-256 hashing rule.

The same customer identifier receives the same selection decision.

All October events belonging to selected customers were retained.

The sample was generated by scanning the complete original dataset rather than taking only the first portion of the file.

## 6. Sampling Validation

The following checks were completed:

- All 31 October calendar dates are represented.
- All 60,939 sampled customers satisfy the selection rule.
- No sampled customer failed the selection-rule validation.
- No sampled customer identifier is missing.
- An independent scan of all 42,448,764 source records found 849,220 events belonging to selected customers.
- The saved analytical sample contains 849,220 events.
- Per-customer source and sample event counts match for every selected customer.
- Zero customer-level event-count mismatches were detected.

**Result:** Customer selection and event-count retention validations passed.

## 7. Analytical Limitations

1. The dataset represents historical behavior from October 2019, so findings may not reflect current customer behavior.
2. The analytical sample is a subset of the complete dataset and may exhibit sampling variation.
3. A 2% customer-selection rule does not guarantee an exactly 2% share of customers or events.
4. Missing category and brand information may limit some segmentation analyses.
5. Observed activity is restricted to October; customer journeys may begin before or continue after the analysis period.
6. The dataset does not directly establish why customers abandon purchases.
7. Funnel conversion rates must be calculated using a clearly defined customer, session, or product-level denominator.
8. Event counts are not equivalent to unique customer counts.
9. Event-count reconciliation does not independently establish row-level identity.
10. Revenue-related metrics require explicit assumptions about purchase-event prices and quantities.

## 9. Dataset Selection Decision

**Decision: Selected for the project.**

The dataset provides the core behavioral events needed to investigate e-commerce conversion funnels.

It is suitable for customer-journey analysis, subject to validation of event ordering, session behavior, funnel definitions, and revenue assumptions in subsequent project steps.
